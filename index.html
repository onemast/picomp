    About Picomp

    Picomp makes images smaller and converts them between formats, right in your browser.

    Private by design

    Your images stay on your device, and there is no account. All the work happens on your own computer or phone, so it is just as safe for personal photos as for confidential files.

    How it works

    Picomp runs the same open-source encoders that professional tools rely on, including MozJPEG, libwebp, libaom and libjxl, compiled to run inside the browser. Each image is encoded, compared with the original and adjusted, so a photo and a flat graphic each get the settings that suit them instead of one fixed quality for everything.

    Why Picomp

    • Instant start. Big files start straight away, and private ones stay private.
    • Smaller files. In side-by-side tests against popular online compressors, Picomp's JPG and WebP output came out smaller on most samples at comparable quality.
    • Modern formats. Saves AVIF and JXL as well as JPG, PNG and WebP, and opens iPhone HEIC photos.
    • No catches. No sign-up, no daily limits and no watermarks.
