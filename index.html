Picomp

Camera, lens and date. Also the GPS location, if the photo has one.
px
    JPG
    Photos. Smallest for anything camera-like, no transparency. Works everywhere.
    PNG
    Logos, screenshots, graphics with flat colour or transparency. Photos get big.
    WebP
    Photos and graphics, with transparency. 25% smaller than JPG. Works in every browser.
    AVIF
    Newer than WebP, 20 to 30% smaller again, slower to make. Works in every current browser.
    JXL
    Best quality per byte, but only Safari can display it so far. Good for archiving, not for the web.
